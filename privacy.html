Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains how New Storia Magic LLC ("Storia Kids", "we") handles personal information.

What we collect

When you order through WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger or Instagram, we collect what you send us: your name, phone number, delivery address, and your child's name, age and photo, plus the messages in the conversation.

How we use it

Only to create, deliver and support your order, to send you updates about it, and to improve our service. Children's photos are used only to create the requested book.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with providers we need to fulfil your order, such as payment providers, printing and delivery partners, and the messaging platforms you contact us on.

Retention and your rights

We keep information only as long as needed for your order and our legal obligations. You can ask us at any time to access or delete your information, including your child's photo, by emailing info@storiagate.com or messaging us on WhatsApp.
